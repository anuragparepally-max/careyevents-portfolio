Event Overview

NFL Honors stands as professional football's premier awards ceremony, celebrating the league's most outstanding players and performances each season. Since 2011, Carey International has been the exclusive ground transportation provider for this prestigious event, deploying 350 vehicles annually to serve 2,000 attendees including NFL players, coaches, executives, media, and celebrity guests.

The event, held the night before the Super Bowl, requires flawless execution in a high-pressure environment. Our team coordinates transportation for MVP candidates, Hall of Fame inductees, league officials, broadcast talent, and VIP sponsors across multiple hotels and venues. Working in close partnership with NFL event staff and security teams, we manage complex logistics including red carpet arrivals, after-party departures, and late-night hotel returns.

Over 13 consecutive years, Carey has maintained a perfect track record of on-time performance, ensuring that football's biggest stars arrive ready to celebrate the sport's greatest achievements. Our experienced chauffeurs provide the discretion, professionalism, and reliability expected for the NFL's most elegant evening, making us the trusted transportation partner for this annual tradition.
