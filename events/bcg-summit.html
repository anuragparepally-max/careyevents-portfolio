Event Overview

Boston Consulting Group's Annual Summit brings together 500 senior executives and partners from around the world for strategic discussions and thought leadership sessions. Carey International provides comprehensive ground transportation for this prestigious event, deploying 150 vehicles to ensure seamless coordination for BCG's global leadership team, guest speakers, and client executives.

The multi-day summit requires precise logistics management, coordinating airport arrivals from international destinations, hotel transfers, conference venue shuttles, and evening event transportation. Our team works closely with BCG's event coordinators to manage complex scheduling, accommodate last-minute changes, and provide the executive-level service expected by senior consulting professionals. With many executives arriving from global offices, we ensure smooth transitions and reliable transportation throughout the summit.

Carey's proven track record of excellence with BCG's annual summit demonstrates our ability to deliver consistent, high-quality transportation for executive audiences. Our professional chauffeurs understand the importance of confidentiality, punctuality, and discretion when transporting consulting leaders and their clients. Perfect on-time performance ensures that summit sessions begin promptly and executives can maximize their time at this important annual gathering.
