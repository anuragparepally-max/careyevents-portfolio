Event Overview

Louis Vuitton's Cruise Collection shows represent fashion at its most spectacular, combining high design with breathtaking venues. Carey International has been the trusted transportation partner for these prestigious events, deploying 350 vehicles across three iconic shows: New York 2019, San Diego 2023, and Palm Springs. We provided comprehensive ground transportation for models, designers, celebrities, fashion editors, VIP guests, and production teams.

Each Louis Vuitton Cruise show presents unique logistical challenges, from managing multiple hotel pickups to coordinating runway timing to the minute. Our team worked closely with Louis Vuitton event producers to ensure seamless coordination for hundreds of international guests, including A-list celebrities, influential fashion editors, and luxury brand executives. From airport arrivals to hotel transfers, venue shuttles to after-party departures, we maintained the elegance and precision expected by one of the world's most prestigious luxury brands.

Operating across three different cities showcased Carey's ability to deliver consistent, world-class service regardless of location. Our fleet of luxury vehicles, professional chauffeurs, and meticulous attention to detail ensured that every guest arrived in style, on time, and ready to experience Louis Vuitton's artistic vision. Perfect on-time performance across all three events solidified our position as the transportation partner of choice for luxury fashion's most important moments.
