Event Overview

The Global Citizen Concert in New York City brought together some of the world's biggest musical acts for a historic event promoting social change. Carey International was selected to manage all main stage talent transportation, deploying 250 vehicles to ensure seamless coordination for performers, crew, production staff, and VIP guests throughout this high-profile event.

Operating in the heart of Manhattan required precision logistics, navigating complex traffic patterns, security protocols, and strict timing requirements. Our team coordinated with event producers, security personnel, and venue management to ensure every artist arrived on time, every time. From airport pickups to backstage arrivals, we provided white-glove service for A-list talent while managing crew and production staff transport with equal attention to detail.

The event showcased Carey's ability to execute flawless transportation logistics for large-scale, high-visibility events where timing is critical and discretion is paramount. Our 100% on-time performance ensured that show schedules were maintained and performers were able to focus on delivering powerful performances to a global audience.
