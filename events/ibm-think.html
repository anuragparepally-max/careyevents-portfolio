Event Overview

IBM Think Conference represents the premier gathering for enterprise technology leaders, bringing together 1,100 CIOs, CTOs, developers, and business strategists to explore AI, cloud computing, and digital transformation. Carey International provided comprehensive ground transportation for this flagship event, deploying 300 vehicles to serve IBM's clients, partners, and technology executives throughout the multi-day conference.

The conference demanded sophisticated logistics coordination to manage transportation between keynote sessions, technical workshops, customer roundtables, and networking events. Our team worked in close partnership with IBM's event coordinators to ensure seamless movement for enterprise executives attending this important annual gathering. With attendees arriving from global corporations and sessions running from early morning through evening receptions, reliable transportation was essential for maximizing the value of this strategic conference.

Carey's deep expertise in the enterprise technology sector and proven track record with large-scale corporate events made us the natural choice for IBM Think transportation. Our professional chauffeurs understand the importance of confidentiality when transporting executives discussing strategic IT initiatives and digital transformation plans. Perfect on-time performance throughout the conference ensured attendees could focus on learning about emerging technologies, networking with industry peers, and advancing their organizations' innovation agendas.
