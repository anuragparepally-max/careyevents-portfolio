Event Overview

When rock legends Foo Fighters took their electrifying show across Europe, Carey International orchestrated a transportation operation as powerful as their performances. Deploying 500 vehicles across 35 tour dates in 14 countries, we provided comprehensive ground transportation for the band, crew, production staff, and special guests throughout the entire tour.

From stadium shows to festival headlining slots, our fleet ensured seamless coordination across multiple cities and venues. Working closely with tour management, we navigated complex logistics including equipment transport, crew shuttles, and VIP transfers while maintaining the highest standards of service and achieving perfect on-time performance.

Our experienced team coordinated with local partners across Europe, managing everything from airport pickups to venue arrivals, ensuring that every member of the touring party arrived refreshed and ready to deliver unforgettable performances night after night.
