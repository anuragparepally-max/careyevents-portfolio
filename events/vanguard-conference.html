Event Overview

Vanguard's Leadership Conference assembles 450 senior executives from one of the world's largest investment management companies for strategic planning and leadership development. Carey International provides exclusive ground transportation for this prestigious gathering, deploying 150 vehicles to serve Vanguard's executive leadership, senior portfolio managers, and key stakeholders throughout the multi-day conference.

The conference requires meticulous coordination of transportation between conference venues, hotels, and evening events. Our team works in close partnership with Vanguard's event coordinators to manage complex schedules, accommodate executives arriving from multiple regional offices, and provide the premium service expected by leaders in the financial services industry. With tight session schedules and networking opportunities, perfect timing is essential to maximize the value of this important annual gathering.

Carey's commitment to excellence aligns with Vanguard's values of integrity and client service. Our professional chauffeurs understand the importance of confidentiality when transporting senior financial executives, and our fleet of premium vehicles reflects the quality standards expected by Vanguard leadership. Consistent on-time performance ensures that conference sessions, workshops, and networking events proceed smoothly, allowing executives to focus on strategic priorities and leadership development.
