Event Overview

Gucci's New York events showcase Italian fashion heritage meeting modern luxury, and Carey International has been the trusted ground transportation partner for these prestigious occasions. Deploying 200 vehicles across multiple events, we provided comprehensive transportation for fashion industry leaders, celebrities, influencers, media representatives, and VIP guests attending Gucci's exclusive New York presentations, store openings, and brand celebrations.

Each Gucci event in Manhattan demands precision logistics in one of the world's most challenging urban environments. Our team coordinated seamless transportation from multiple hotel locations to exclusive venues, managing tight schedules, security protocols, and the high expectations of luxury fashion clientele. Working closely with Gucci's event team, we ensured that every guest experienced the same elegance in their transportation as they would expect from the iconic Italian brand.

Our fleet of immaculate luxury vehicles and professionally trained chauffeurs reflected Gucci's commitment to excellence. From airport arrivals to hotel transfers, venue shuttles to late-night departures, we maintained perfect on-time performance while providing the discretion and sophistication required for high-profile fashion events. Carey's proven ability to deliver flawless execution in New York's complex environment has made us the transportation partner of choice for Gucci's most important moments.
