Event Overview

NBA All-Star Weekend represents basketball's premier celebration, bringing together the world's greatest players, coaches, executives, sponsors, and media for four days of high-profile events. Carey International orchestrated comprehensive ground transportation, deploying 500 vehicles to serve 3,000 attendees including players, team personnel, league officials, VIPs, and media representatives.

The logistics challenge was immense: coordinating transportation for All-Star Game participants, Skills Challenge competitors, Three-Point Contest shooters, Slam Dunk contestants, celebrity game players, and hundreds of special guests across multiple venues. Our fleet managed everything from private jet arrivals to hotel transfers, practice facility shuttles, arena arrivals, and late-night event departures with precision timing.

Working closely with NBA event coordinators and security teams, Carey delivered flawless execution throughout the weekend. Our experienced chauffeurs navigated complex traffic patterns, managed tight schedules, and provided the discretion and professionalism expected for basketball's elite. Perfect on-time performance ensured every event ran smoothly and every participant arrived ready to perform on the sport's biggest stage.
