Event Overview

NVIDIA's GPU Technology Conference (GTC) represents the premier gathering for AI, graphics, and accelerated computing innovation, bringing together 1,200 researchers, developers, executives, and technology leaders. Carey International provided comprehensive ground transportation for this groundbreaking event, deploying 300 vehicles to serve attendees from leading tech companies, research institutions, and AI startups.

The multi-day conference demanded precise coordination to manage transportation between keynote presentations, technical sessions, hands-on labs, and networking events. Our team worked closely with NVIDIA's event coordinators to ensure seamless movement for executives, researchers, and developers attending this flagship technology event. With sessions running from early morning through evening receptions, reliable transportation was essential for maximizing attendee engagement with cutting-edge AI and computing innovations.

Carey's expertise in technology sector events made us the natural choice for NVIDIA GTC transportation. Our professional chauffeurs understand the importance of discretion when transporting executives discussing proprietary technology developments, and our fleet of premium vehicles reflects the innovation leadership that defines NVIDIA. Perfect on-time performance throughout the conference ensured attendees could focus on learning, networking, and advancing the future of AI and computing.
