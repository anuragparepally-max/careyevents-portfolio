Event Overview

When Louis Vuitton hosts a fashion show, every detail matters—from the runway to the ride. Carey International was entrusted with providing exclusive ground transportation for the Louis Vuitton Men's Pre-Fall Show in Miami, deploying 400 premium SUVs over five days to ensure flawless service for designers, models, celebrities, fashion editors, and high-net-worth attendees.

This wasn't simply transportation—it was an extension of the Louis Vuitton brand experience. Our fleet of immaculate, late-model SUVs, driven by professionally trained chauffeurs in tailored uniforms, delivered white-glove service that matched the sophistication of the event itself. With 20 vehicles dedicated exclusively to talent transport, we ensured every VIP arrived refreshed, on time, and ready for the spotlight.
