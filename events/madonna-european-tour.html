Event Overview

When one of the world's most iconic performers embarks on a European tour, the logistics must match the spectacle. Carey International orchestrated transportation for Madonna's ambitious multi-city European tour, deploying 700 vehicles across 15 countries to ensure seamless movement for talent, crew, VIPs, and production staff.

The challenge was immense: coordinating luxury transportation across multiple languages, time zones, and regulatory environments while maintaining the white-glove service expected by A-list talent. Our team executed flawlessly, managing everything from airport transfers to venue shuttles, ensuring every member of the tour arrived on time, every time.

Execution Timeline

Pre-Production (4 Months Prior)

Carey's European operations team conducted comprehensive route planning, vendor partnerships, and regulatory compliance across all 15 countries. Established local contacts and backup protocols in each city.

Logistics Coordination (2 Months Prior)

Finalized vehicle specifications, driver assignments, and communication protocols. Conducted security briefings and rehearsals with tour management to ensure seamless coordination.

Tour Execution (6 Months)

700 vehicles deployed across 45 tour stops spanning Paris, London, Berlin, Amsterdam, Rome, Barcelona, and more. Zero incidents, 100% on-time performance, and overwhelmingly positive feedback from tour management.

Post-Tour Analysis

Comprehensive debrief with tour management, documenting lessons learned and best practices. Solidified relationship for future European touring needs.
