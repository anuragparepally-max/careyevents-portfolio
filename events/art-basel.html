Event Overview

Art World Excellence

Art Basel Miami Beach transforms Miami into the epicenter of the international art world each December. As the premier art fair in the Americas, this event attracts the world's most discerning collectors, gallery owners, artists, and cultural influencers.

We provided comprehensive transportation services coordinating collector shuttles between the Miami Beach Convention Center, satellite fairs including Design Miami, gallery openings across Wynwood and the Design District, and exclusive VIP parties throughout Miami Beach with concierge-level service.
