Event Overview

PGA Tour events demand the same precision and excellence that define professional golf. Carey International provided comprehensive ground transportation for tournament week, deploying 300 vehicles to serve 1,500 participants including touring professionals, caddies, PGA officials, sponsors, media, and VIP guests across multiple tournament venues and practice facilities.

Throughout the tournament week, our fleet managed complex logistics including early morning practice round transfers, pro-am event shuttles, tournament day transportation, and sponsor hospitality coordination. Working closely with PGA Tour operations staff, we ensured seamless movement between hotels, golf courses, driving ranges, and evening events while maintaining the quiet professionalism and discretion expected in the world of professional golf.

Our experienced team understands the unique demands of golf tournaments: tight morning tee times, precise timing for player arrivals, sponsor expectations, and the need for quiet, comfortable transportation that allows players to focus on their game. Perfect on-time performance throughout tournament week ensured that every participant arrived refreshed and ready to compete at the highest level.
