Event Overview

BlackRock's Annual Summit brings together 500 senior executives from the world's largest asset management firm for strategic discussions and leadership alignment. Carey International provides comprehensive ground transportation for this premier event, deploying 150 vehicles to serve BlackRock's global leadership team, portfolio managers, and senior stakeholders throughout the multi-day summit.

The summit demands precision logistics to coordinate executives arriving from international offices, manage complex scheduling across multiple venues, and provide seamless transportation for formal sessions, breakout meetings, and networking events. Our team works closely with BlackRock's event coordinators to ensure flawless execution, accommodating last-minute schedule changes and maintaining the executive-level service expected by leaders managing trillions in global assets.

Carey's reputation for excellence in the financial services sector makes us the natural choice for BlackRock's most important annual gathering. Our professional chauffeurs understand the critical importance of confidentiality and discretion when transporting senior financial executives, and our premium fleet reflects BlackRock's commitment to quality. Perfect on-time performance throughout the summit ensures that strategic sessions proceed as planned, allowing executives to maximize the value of this crucial annual event.
