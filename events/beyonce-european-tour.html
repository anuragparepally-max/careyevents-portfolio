Event Overview

Redefining Global Tour Logistics

Beyoncé's European tour represented the single largest ground transportation deployment in Carey International's history. Spanning 18 countries, 52 tour dates, and 800 meticulously coordinated vehicles, this operation redefined what's possible in international touring logistics.

From stadiums in London to arenas in Barcelona, concert halls in Paris to festival grounds in Amsterdam, our team orchestrated seamless transportation for performers, crew, production staff, and VIP guests. The complexity was staggering: managing cross-border regulations, coordinating with local vendors in multiple languages, and ensuring every person arrived on time, every time—all while maintaining the discretion and luxury expected by one of the world's biggest stars.

Fleet Composition

Premium Vehicles for Every Need

Premium SUVs

500 luxury SUVs including Mercedes GLS, BMW X7, and Range Rover models for talent, management, and VIP transport. Each vehicle outfitted with tinted windows, premium sound systems, and climate control.

Sprinter Vans

220 Mercedes Sprinter vans configured for crew transport, wardrobe management, and equipment logistics. Wi-Fi enabled with power outlets for mobile productivity during transit.

Tour Coaches

80 executive coach buses for dancers, backup singers, and technical crew. Luxury seating, entertainment systems, and refreshment areas for comfortable long-distance travel between cities.
