Event Overview

Oracle OpenWorld in San Francisco stands as one of the world's largest enterprise technology conferences, bringing together 1,500 IT executives, developers, database administrators, and cloud computing professionals. Carey International provided comprehensive ground transportation for this massive event, deploying 300 vehicles throughout the week-long conference to serve Oracle customers, partners, and technology leaders from around the globe.

The conference demanded exceptional coordination to manage transportation across multiple downtown San Francisco venues, with attendees navigating between keynote presentations, technical sessions, hands-on labs, and customer appreciation events. Our team worked closely with Oracle's event coordinators to ensure seamless movement for CIOs, CTOs, and IT directors attending this flagship technology gathering. With sessions running from morning through evening events at venues across the city, reliable transportation was essential for maximizing the value of this important annual conference.

Carey's proven expertise in large-scale technology events and deep knowledge of San Francisco logistics made us the ideal transportation partner for Oracle OpenWorld. Our professional chauffeurs understand the enterprise technology sector and provide the discretion required when transporting executives discussing strategic IT initiatives. Perfect on-time performance throughout the conference ensured attendees could focus on learning about cloud innovations, networking with peers, and maximizing their OpenWorld experience.
