Event Overview

The Super Bowl is more than a football game—it's a week-long celebration of sports, entertainment, and corporate hospitality that attracts the world's elite. Carey International served as the exclusive ground transportation partner, deploying 1,200 vehicles to serve 5,000 VIPs, corporate sponsors, celebrity guests, and NFL officials during Super Bowl week.

From Fortune 500 CEOs to A-list entertainers, from team owners to halftime performers, our fleet delivered white-glove service around the clock. We coordinated airport pickups, hotel transfers, stadium shuttles, exclusive parties, and corporate events with military precision, ensuring every passenger arrived on time and in style.
